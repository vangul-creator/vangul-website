ABOUT

VANGUL

vangul - is a young independent music artist born in Kyiv in 2004.

His music blends electronic sounds, funk, phonk, and hip-hop. Each track is created to capture a feeling — from positive energy and freedom to adrenaline and tension.

VANGUL finds his biggest inspiration in life, everyday moments, and the simple feeling of enjoying the moment.

He does not seek constant attention and prefers to remain somewhat anonymous, letting the music speak for itself.

The goal is simple — keep creating, keep developing, and reach more people through the music.
